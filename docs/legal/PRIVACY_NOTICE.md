## 2. PDFlex document-processing posture

PDFlex is designed primarily as a local desktop application. The PDFs, images,
Word files, signatures, OCR text, temporary files and generated outputs selected
by the user are processed on the user's computer unless a separately configured
feature, integration, support process or cloud service expressly sends data
outside the device.

OCMX does not intentionally collect customer document contents through PDFlex's
core local tools. Users remain responsible for the documents they choose to
process, store, export, share or send for support.

## 3. Personal data we may collect

Depending on the sale channel, license system and support workflow, OCMX may
collect:

- identification and contact data: name, company, email, phone and billing
  contact;
- commercial data: order, invoice, payment status, license plan, renewal and
  support entitlement;
- license and activation data: license key or license ID, activation status,
  application version, device or installation identifier, activation date and
  approximate region;
- technical support data: operating system, application logs, error messages,
  screenshots or files voluntarily provided by the customer;
- update/security data: application version, installer version, update channel,
  download metadata and integrity verification results.

PDFlex should not collect sensitive personal data unless the user voluntarily
includes that information in support material or documents provided to OCMX.

## 4. Purposes of processing

OCMX may process personal data for the following primary purposes:

- sell, activate, renew and administer PDFlex licenses;
- provide technical support and customer service;
- deliver updates, security fixes and release notices;
- prevent abuse, piracy, unauthorized activation or license sharing;
- issue invoices, comply with tax/accounting duties and keep business records;
- diagnose crashes or compatibility issues when the customer requests support;
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

Secondary purposes, where permitted by law and customer preferences, may include:

- sending product tips, renewal reminders or commercial communications related
  to PDFlex;
- improving product planning through aggregated, non-document analytics.

## 5. Transfers and processors

OCMX may share personal data with service providers only as needed for the
purposes above, such as payment processors, invoicing providers, email/support
tools, update hosting, license infrastructure, accounting providers or legal
advisors.

OCMX does not sell customer document contents. If OCMX later adds cloud OCR,
cloud storage, telemetry or online collaboration, the notice must be updated
before enabling those features.

## 6. Retention

OCMX keeps personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes
described in this notice, including support, license administration, fraud
prevention, accounting, tax, legal and audit obligations.

Support files or sample documents voluntarily provided by customers should be
deleted or returned after the support case is resolved, unless retention is
required by law or separately agreed with the customer.

## 7. Security

OCMX uses reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards
appropriate to the nature of the data, such as access controls, limited support
handling, secure transfer channels where available and integrity checks for
installer/update artifacts.

No system is perfectly secure. Customers should keep their own backups, protect
their license keys and avoid sending confidential documents to support unless
strictly necessary.

and OCMX does not knowingly collect children's personal data through PDFlex.
